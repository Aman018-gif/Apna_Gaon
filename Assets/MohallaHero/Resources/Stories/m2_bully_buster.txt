// ================================================================================================
//  CHAPTER 2 · BULLY BUSTER
//  Giver: Pintu
//  Teaches: stop bullying safely (no fighting), get a trusted adult, report it, support the victim,
//           and give the bully a way to change.
//
//  Flow: m2_pintu → m2_fight (Golu or Bunty in the park) → m2_asha (report) → m2_golu (support)
//        → m2_bunty → m2_done
// ================================================================================================

=== m2_pintu ===
Pintu: Bhaiya, emergency! Meme nahi, sach mein emergency.
Pintu: Bunty roz Golu ko park mein pareshan karta hai. Uska tiffin chheen leta hai, drawings phaad deta hai. Aaj bhi wahi ho raha hai, park ke upar-left kone mein.
Pintu: Main chhota hoon, Bunty mujhse bada hai. Tum chaloge?
* [Haan, abhi chalte hain. Par ladai nahi karenge.] #courage:+5 #civic:+2 #why:Bystanders are powerful: most bullying stops within seconds when someone steps in calmly. Saying "no fighting" upfront keeps everyone safe.
    You: Haan, abhi chalte hain. Par ladai nahi karenge, samjhe?
    Pintu: Deal! Calm mode ON.
* [Bachchon ka jhagda hai, apne aap theek ho jayega.] #courage:-4 #civic:-2 #why:Bullying rarely stops on its own. It usually gets worse when nobody reacts, because the bully learns nobody will.
    You: Bachchon ka jhagda hai, khud theek ho jayega.
    Pintu: Roz ho raha hai, bhaiya. Roz. Golu ab school jaane se darta hai.
    You: ...theek hai. Chalo, dekhte hain.
- ~ m2_started = 1
Pintu: Park ke upar-left kone mein. Jaldi!
-> END

=== m2_fight ===
In the corner of Gulmohar Park, Bunty is holding Golu's drawing book high above his head. Golu is close to tears.
Bunty: Arre Golu-polu, yeh kya bakwaas drawing hai? Superhero? Tu? Hahaha! Phaad doon?
Golu: Please wapas de do... meri mummy ne woh notebook di thi...
* [Beech mein khade ho jao, calmly: "Bunty, book wapas karo."] #courage:+8 #civic:+3 #why:Calm, firm words and your body between them (without touching anyone) is the safest way to interrupt bullying. You took away the audience the bully wanted.
    You: Bunty. Book wapas karo. Yeh mazaak nahi hai, aur tum yeh jaante ho.
    Bunty: Tu kaun hai be? ...Achha theek hai, le. Boring log.
    Bunty throws the notebook on the bench and stomps off.
    ~ m2_stopped = 1
* [Zor se Asha Ma'am ko awaaz do / kisi bade ko bulao] #courage:+4 #civic:+5 #why:Getting a trusted adult is not "snitching", it's smart. Adults can stop it AND fix it later. Your safety matters too.
    You: ASHA MA'AM! Yahan aaiye, park mein!
    Bunty: Arre, teacher! Bhaago!
    Bunty drops the notebook and runs. Golu picks it up, shaking.
    ~ m2_stopped = 1
* [Usse dhakka do: "Ruk ja, nahi toh maarunga!"] #courage:+2 #civic:-8 #why:Fighting turns one bully into two fighters. Someone gets hurt, both get punished, and the bully gets an excuse to come back angrier. Stop it without violence.
    You push Bunty hard. He stumbles, falls on the gravel and scrapes his knee.
    Bunty: Aaah! Tu... tujhe dekh lunga! Main sabko bataunga tune maara!
    Bunty limps away with a bleeding knee. Golu looks even more scared now.
    ~ m2_fought = 1
    ~ m2_stopped = 1
* [Phone nikaalo, video banao: meme material!] #civic:-5 #honesty:-2 #why:Filming someone being bullied and sharing it humiliates them again, in front of everyone, forever. Never record to laugh. If you record, do it only as evidence for an adult.
    You: Ruko, video bana leta hoon! Viral hoga!
    Pintu: Bhaiya?! Yeh meme nahi hai! Golu ro raha hai!
    Bunty: Haha, dekho, tera bhi video ban raha hai Golu!
    You feel terrible. You put the phone away. Bunty is still holding the book...
-> END

=== m2_asha ===
Asha Ma'am: {player_name}? Tum pareshan lag rahe ho. Kya hua?
* [Sab sach-sach batao, jo dekha wahi] #honesty:+5 #civic:+5 #why:Reporting exactly what you saw, no more, no less, helps adults act fairly. True reports get taken seriously.
    You: Ma'am, Bunty roz Golu ka tiffin chheenta hai aur drawings phaadta hai. Aaj park mein bhi yahi ho raha tha.
    {m2_fought: You: Aur... maine Bunty ko dhakka de diya. Galti thi. Woh gir gaya.}
    Asha Ma'am: Tumne sach bataya, yeh bahut badi baat hai. Main dono ke parents se baat karungi.
* [Thoda badha-chadha ke: "Bunty ne Golu ko bahut maara!"] #honesty:-5 #civic:+2 #why:Exaggerating feels like it helps, but when adults find out the details were wrong, they start doubting the whole story, and the victim pays the price.
    You: Ma'am, Bunty ne Golu ko bahut buri tarah maara! Khoon nikal aaya!
    Asha Ma'am: Khoon? Main abhi Golu se milti hoon...
    Asha Ma'am: Golu keh raha hai maara nahi, bas book chheeni. {player_name}, sach bolna zaroori hai, warna log asli baat pe bhi shak karte hain.
* [Kuch nahi Ma'am, bas mazaak chal raha tha.] #courage:-3 #civic:-3 #why:Calling bullying "just a joke" protects the bully, not the victim. If it's a joke, everyone is laughing. Was Golu laughing?
    You: Kuch nahi Ma'am. Bas bachchon ka mazaak tha.
    Asha Ma'am: Achha? Theek hai... par kuch ho toh mujhe zaroor batana.
    Pintu: (whispering) Bhaiya... Golu ke liye. Please.
    -> END
- Asha Ma'am: Hum school mein ek "Dost Circle" shuru karenge: jahan bachche bina dare baat kar sakein. Aur Bunty se bhi baat hogi, usse bhi samajhna padega.
~ m2_reported = 1
Asha Ma'am: Ab Golu ke paas jao. Use ek dost ki zaroorat hai, hero ki nahi.
-> END

=== m2_golu ===
Golu is sitting alone, holding his crumpled notebook.
Golu: Thank you... Par kal phir hoga. Hamesha hota hai.
* ["Lunch saath karein? Mujhe apni comics dikhao!"] #civic:+4 #courage:+2 #trust:+20 #why:Victims of bullying often feel alone. A friend at lunch makes them far less likely to be targeted, and far more likely to speak up.
    You: Kal se lunch saath karte hain. Aur mujhe tumhari comics dikhao, superhero wali!
    Golu: Sach mein? Maine ek comic banaya hai... "Captain Kachra-Saaf"! Tum pe based hai!
* ["Tum bhi thoda strong bano yaar, bolo kuch!"] #civic:-3 #why:Telling a victim to "be stronger" makes it sound like the bullying is their fault. It never is. Support first; confidence grows from feeling safe.
    You: Tum bhi kuch bola karo yaar. Thoda strong bano.
    Golu: ...Main koshish karta hoon. Par mujhe darr lagta hai.
    You: Sorry, Golu. Darrna galat nahi hai. Main saath hoon.
* ["Kal se main tumhare saath school chalunga."] #courage:+3 #civic:+2 #trust:+15 #why:Showing up is support. Bullies pick on people who seem alone; a companion changes that.
    You: Kal se school saath chalenge. Dekhte hain koi kya karta hai.
    Golu: Okay! Main subah 8 baje gate pe milunga.
- ~ m2_supported = 1
Golu: Waise... Bunty itna bura bhi nahi hai. Uske ghar mein roz jhagde hote hain. Pintu ne bataya.
-> END

=== m2_bunty ===
Bunty is kicking a stone near the park bench. He sees you and scowls.
Bunty: Kya hai? Ab tu bhi lecture dega? Asha Ma'am ne already mere papa ko phone kar diya.
{m2_fought: Bunty: Aur mera ghutna abhi bhi dukh raha hai. Thanks a lot.}
* [Baith jao. "Tum aisa kyun karte ho?" Sach mein suno.] #civic:+5 #courage:+3 #trust:+20 #why:Many children who bully are dealing with something painful themselves. Listening doesn't excuse bullying, but it's often the start of stopping it for good.
    You: Lecture nahi. Bas pooch raha hoon: tum aisa kyun karte ho?
    Bunty: ...Ghar pe sab chillaate rehte hain. Yahan aake... main chillaata hoon. Pata nahi kyun.
    You: Golu se maafi maangoge? Aur park mein cricket? Golu score badhiya likhta hai.
    Bunty: ...Theek hai. Par agar tune kisi ko bataya ki main roya, toh...
    You: Kaun roya? Main toh sirf cricket ki baat kar raha tha.
* ["Tum bure insaan ho, Bunty."] #civic:-3 #trust:-10 #why:Labelling someone "bad" gives them nothing to change into. Name the behaviour ("snatching books is wrong") and leave room for the person to do better.
    You: Tum bure insaan ho, Bunty. Bas.
    Bunty: Haan, sab yahi kehte hain. Toh phir main bura hi sahi.
* ["Agli baar kiya toh seedha Principal ke paas."] #civic:+1 #courage:+1 #why:Clear consequences are fair and useful, but threats alone rarely change why someone behaves badly.
    You: Agli baar kisi ko pareshan kiya, toh seedha Principal ke paas jaunga.
    Bunty: Haan haan, pata hai. Ja.
- ~ m2_done = 1
Pintu: Chapter complete: "Bully Buster"! Waise, Sharma Aunty tumhe dhoondh rahi thi. Building ke bahar.
-> END
