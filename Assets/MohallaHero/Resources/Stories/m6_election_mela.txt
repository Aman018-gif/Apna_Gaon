=== jugaad_bribe ===
A party worker leans in with an envelope and a shiny new pressure cooker.
Party worker: Bhaiya, ₹500 aur yeh pressure cooker. Bas Rocket pe button dabana hai. Kisi ko pata nahi chalega.
* [Mana karo: "Mera vote bikta nahi."] #honesty:+8 #courage:+4 #why:A vote sold for ₹500 is worth about 27 paise a day over five years. Saying no keeps your vote, and your voice, yours.
    You: Mera vote bikta nahi, bhaiya. Cooker aap hi rakho.
    Party worker: Arre, pressure cooker hai! Seeti bhi bajta hai! ...Theek hai, theek hai.
    ~ bribe_refused = 1
* [Le lo: "Paise le leta hoon, vote toh secret hai"] #honesty:-15 #civic:-8 #why:Accepting money or gifts for your vote is a crime under election law, for the giver and the taker. Even if you vote differently, you've told corrupt candidates that votes can be bought.
    ~ earn(500)
    ~ bribe_taken = 1
    You: Theek hai, de do. Vote toh secret hai, kisko dunga kise pata.
    Party worker: Smart! Hehe. Rocket yaad rakhna!
    The pressure cooker feels heavier than it should.
* [Officer Fernandes ko report karo] #civic:+8 #courage:+6 #why:Vote-buying thrives because people stay quiet. Reporting it to the Election Officer (or on the Election Commission's complaint app) gets it stopped, for everyone.
    You: Main yeh Officer Fernandes ko bata raha hoon. Abhi.
    Party worker: Arre arre! Galti ho gayi! Main toh... samosa baant raha tha!
    Officer Fernandes arrives two minutes later and confiscates the envelope. The cooker goes into the "evidence" pile.
    ~ bribe_reported = 1
    ~ bribe_refused = 1
-> END

Actor Ramu: Ek din ka... 27 paise?! Mera vote 27 paise mein bika?!
Sutradhar: Ab sawaal audience se! Natak ka sabak kya hai?
* [Vote ki keemat paison se nahi naapi ja sakti.] #civic:+4 #honesty:+2 #why:Your vote decides who controls budgets worth crores for five years. No gift can be worth more than that.
    You: Vote ki keemat paison se nahi naapi ja sakti!
    Sutradhar: Sahi jawab! Taaliyaan!
* [Neta se kaam ka hisaab maango, gift nahi.] #civic:+4 #courage:+2 #why:Accountability means asking "what did you do with the power we gave you?", in meetings, through RTI and at the ballot box.

    Sutradhar: Ekdum sahi! Hisaab do, hisaab do!
