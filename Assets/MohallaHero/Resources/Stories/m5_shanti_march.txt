=== m5_meera_gather ===
Meera Didi: Teen placards! Waah. Ab sabko Gulmohar Park bulaate hain. Shaam ho rahi hai, diye jalayenge.
* [Chalo, sabko bulao. March shuru karte hain!]
    You: Chalo, sabko bulao. March shuru karte hain!
* [Ek minute, thoda tayyar ho jaun.]
    Meera Didi: Theek hai. Jab ready ho, mujhse baat karna.
    -> END
- ~ protest("gather")
~ give("petition")
The sun sets over Gulmohar Park. One by one, your neighbours arrive with diyas in their hands. {joined()} of them joined because you convinced them, and they brought their families.
Meera Didi: Sab aa gaye! Petition pe sabke signature ho gaye. {player_name}, march se pehle do shabd bolo.
* [Facts ke saath: "Humare paas photo, bill aur RTI hai. Hum shanti se hisaab maangenge."] #civic:+5 #courage:+5 #why:Leaders who stick to verified facts and peaceful methods keep the moral high ground, and the media, the officials and fence-sitters notice.
    You: Humare paas photo hai, bill hai, aur sarkaar ka apna RTI reply hai. Hum kisi ke ghar nahi jaa rahe; hum Ward Office jaa rahe hain, hisaab maangne. Shanti se!
    Crowd: Hisaab do! Hisaab do!
* [Josh mein: "Chalo, Netaji ka ghar gher lete hain!"] #civic:-5 #courage:+1 #why:Surrounding someone's home is intimidation, not protest. It turns a fight against corruption into a fight against a person, and gives them an easy way to play victim.
    You: Chalo, Netaji ka ghar gher lete hain! Dikhate hain kaun hai boss!
    Meera Didi: Nahi! Hum kisi ke ghar nahi jaate. Hum Ward Office jaate hain, petition dete hain. Yahi hamara raasta hai.
    The crowd murmurs uneasily, then nods along with Meera Didi.
* [Hasaate hue: "Netaji ne chaand pe WiFi diya, hum zameen pe bench maangte hain!"] #courage:+4 #civic:+3 #why:Humour disarms fear and anger. Peaceful movements around the world have used jokes and satire to make people listen.
    You: Netaji ne chaand pe WiFi ka vaada kiya. Hum bas zameen pe ek bench maangte hain!
    The crowd laughs. Even Bunty grins. Pintu films it for his "good memes only" page.
- Meera Didi: Diyon ke raaste chalo: Main Road, phir Gali No. 2, phir Ward Office. Sab tumhare peeche!
~ m5_gathered = 1
~ protest("march")
-> END

=== march_incident ===
Near the posters of Netaji on the wall of Gali No. 2, a young man from the crowd picks up a stone.
Angry man: Bas bahut ho gaya shanti-shanti! Netaji ke posters phaado! Uske office pe patthar maaro!
Some people in the crowd start shouting along. The diyas flicker.
* [Shanti se roko: "Patthar neeche rakho. Humari takat sach hai, patthar nahi."] #courage:+8 #civic:+5 #why:Stopping violence inside your own group takes more courage than shouting at the other side. One stone could have turned "citizens with evidence" into "rioters" in tomorrow's news.
    You: Bhai, patthar neeche rakho. Humari takat sach hai, patthar nahi. Ek patthar chala, toh kal ki news mein hum goonde honge aur Netaji hero.
    Angry man: ...Theek hai. Theek hai. Sorry.
    Meera Didi: (quietly) Shabash.
* [Unke saath milke posters phaado aur patthar maaro] #violence #civic:-15 #courage:-3 #honesty:-3 #why:Vandalism and violence are crimes, even for a good cause. They hurt people, give the corrupt an excuse ("see, troublemakers!") and drive away supporters.
    You tear down a poster. Someone throws a stone. Glass shatters at the party office. Somebody screams.
    Police whistles. Half the crowd runs. Raju's auto has a cracked windshield. Netaji appears on TV that night: "Dekho, yeh hai inka 'shanti march'!"
    Meera Didi: (shaken) Humne kya kiya, {player_name}? Humara sach ab kamzor ho gaya.
* [Kuch mat karo, aage chalte raho] #courage:-3 #why:When something goes wrong in your group, staying silent is also a choice. Here, luckily, others stepped in; next time, it could be you who needs to.
    You keep walking and pretend not to see.
    Behind you, Asha Ma'am steps in and calms the man down. The stone drops. Close call.
- The march goes on, quieter now, the diyas flickering in the evening breeze.
-> END

=== m5_fernandes ===
Officer Fernandes stands at the Ward Office door, looking at the sea of diyas.
Officer Fernandes: Itne log? Raat ke waqt? {violence > 0: Aur mujhe khabar mili hai ki raaste mein tod-phod hui. Yeh theek nahi hua.|Aur poori tarah shanti se. Mujhe aise hi nagrikon ki zaroorat hai.}
* [Petition do: saboot, signatures, aur saaf maang] #civic:+5 #why:A petition is a formal request to an authority. With evidence and signatures attached, the officer is obliged to act on it, and you have a paper trail if they don't.
    ~ take("petition")
    You: Officer, yeh petition hai: park ki photo, golden dustbin ka bill, RTI reply, aur {joined()} parivaaron ke signatures. Hum chahte hain park theek ho aur ₹10 lakh ki jaanch ho.
    Officer Fernandes: Proper format. Proper saboot. Main aaj hi inquiry order karti hoon.
* [Maang karo: "Netaji ko abhi giraftaar karo!"] #civic:-2 #why:Officers can investigate; only courts can punish. Demanding instant punishment skips the fair process that also protects innocent people.
    ~ take("petition")
    You: Netaji ko abhi giraftaar karo!
    Officer Fernandes: Giraftaari courts aur police ka kaam hai, mera nahi. Main inquiry order kar sakti hoon, aur karungi. Yeh petition dijiye.
- {violence > 0: Officer Fernandes: Tod-phod ki bhi alag inquiry hogi. Shanti ke bina, sach bhi shak ke ghere mein aa jata hai.}
Officer Fernandes: Aur ek khabar: agle hafte Ward 7 ka chunav hai. Election Mela kal se Mela Maidan pe! Wahan milte hain.
~ unlock("maidan")
~ m5_done = 1
Pintu: CHAPTER COMPLETE! "Shanti March". Ab Election Mela! Maidan khul gaya hai: south-east corner!
-> END
